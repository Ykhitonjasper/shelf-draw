Shelf Draw

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Shelf Draw (“the app”) handles information when you use it on iPhone.

Information we collect

Shelf Draw does not require an account and does not send your figures, shelves, or placements to our servers. We do not operate an app backend.

Figures, shelf setups, placements, and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

Device access

The app does not request access to your location, camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or any other device data.

Information that may leave your device

Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads this website from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the page under its own privacy terms. Shelf Draw does not use this visit to identify you inside the app.

The support form on the home page opens your email app with the details you entered. Your email provider sends the message directly to our support address; the website does not store a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it using Delete All Data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Because the data is not sent to us, we cannot retrieve or restore it.

Children

Shelf Draw is a general-audience shelf tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
